stagefilewhat it does

Comedic timing: the planner's job

What's tested

  • detectors, planner, placement, camera, the proxy, and render each have unit tests (pnpm test).
  • a testkit.ts builds synthetic perception frames so detectors/planner are tested without a real video.
  • seeded planning makes those tests deterministic.

assignment, revisited

The question

What happens when two people buy the last bag of rice at the same time?

This started as a 3-day take-home with a tight brief: two endpoints, a mandated Controller/Service/Repository layering, and one database transaction. In July I came back to it. I didn't add features; I went deeper on correctness. I fixed three real bugs, wrote a test that proves the concurrency guarantee instead of just asserting it, and added CI with a vulnerability audit.

The proof, in two runs

What I observed on the second pass

  • Negative quantity minted stock. {"quantity": -5} passed stock < quantity, raised stock by 5 and recorded an order priced at −250.
  • Every order was dated year 1. CreatedAt was never assigned, so it stayed DateTime.MinValue. The clock now comes in through TimeProvider.
  • 404 and 409 were the same 400. The service returned bool, so "no such product" and "not enough stock" looked identical to a client.
  • "Clone and run" didn't actually work. Nothing created the SQLite file until migrations moved to startup.

Deliberately not here

Auth, Docker, a queue, caching, an SPA. It's a seeded demo of one narrow problem, and extra infrastructure would only bury the part worth reading.
I also took the original brief PDF out of the repo. It's the assignment author's document, not mine to publish.

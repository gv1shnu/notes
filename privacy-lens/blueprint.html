Request flow

Inside a deep scan

  1. launch headless Chromium (--no-sandbox --disable-dev-shm-usage for containers), viewport 1280×800, desktop Chrome UA
  2. page.evaluateOnNewDocument(fingerprintProbe): runs before any site script
  3. record every request URL; remember the main document's status
  4. goto(url, {waitUntil:"networkidle2", timeout:25000}); on timeout keep going if anything loaded
  5. wait 2.5 s for late pixels
  6. read: probe hits, env (timezone, language, screen, viewport, platform), localStorage + sessionStorage keys, form fields, cookies
  7. cookie is third-party if its domain doesn't end with the page's root domain; persistent if expires > 0
fingerprintProbe wraps: • canvas toDataURL / toBlob / getImageData • WebGL getParameter (GPU) • OfflineAudioContext / AudioContext • navigator getters: hardwareConcurrency, deviceMemory, platform, plugins, languages • screen width / height / colorDepth • getBattery, permissions.query, geolocation • RTCPeerConnection.createDataChannel (local IP) each call → window.__pl.fp.add(label)

The privacy score

score = 100 − 10 × advertising trackers − 12 × session-replay trackers − 5 × analytics / marketing / ab-testing − 1.5 × other third-party hosts − 2 × persistent cookies − 3 × password/payment fields − 6 × fingerprinting probes − 0.5 × storage keys clamp to [3, 100], round

Weights reflect how invasive each thing is: replaying your session or fingerprinting your device costs more than a page-view counter.

floor of 3, not 0: even the worst site is "very bad", not "undefined".

The home page is a stack of layers

The log stream, and why the colours never repeat

There are five colours (green, cyan, amber, red, grey), and up to five entries can be on screen. When the stack is full, the oldest entry gets dropped before the next colour is picked. That way there's always a free colour and no two visible lines ever share one.

I read the used colours back from data-color, not style.color, because the browser hands that back as rgb() instead of the hex I set.

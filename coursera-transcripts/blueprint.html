Blueprint

one script, a handful of well-separated jobs

Run flow

The extraction ladder

extract_transcript tries progressively looser strategies and stops at the first that yields text:

  1. Structured phrases
    If the transcript container has ≥ 3 cue-phrase elements, read each as a cue. Gives clean boundaries and timestamps.
  2. Container innerText
    Otherwise read the whole transcript container's text in one go.
  3. JS scan
    Last-ditch: in-page query for div.rc-Transcript, [data-testid=transcript], or any class containing "ranscript"; keep the longest region.

Cleaning & shaping

sanitize_transcript(): strip 0x200b/c/d, 0xFEFF (zero-width) 0x00A0 (NBSP) → space remove "Play video starting at … and follow transcript" collapse runs of spaces cues_to_outputs(): timestamp line → [mm:ss] prefix raw_to_outputs(): drop bare timestamp lines paragraphize(): ~4 sentences per paragraph

Two outputs are built from the same cues: a clean paragraph form and a timestamped form (emitted only with --timestamps).

Titles are cleaned too: clean_lecture_title strips the "Video• . Duration: 9 min Resume . Click to resume" noise Coursera appends.

slugify + zero-padded numbers keep files in lecture order on disk.

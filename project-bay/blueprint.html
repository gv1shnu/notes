Blueprint

a layered FastAPI backend with a points economy at its core

Backend layers

The pot & proportional payout

pot = creator_stake + Σ challenger_stakes creator proves + all approve: creator takes the whole pot challengers win (proof rejected): each challenger gets back their stake + a share of the creator's stake proportional to stake_i / Σ challenger_stakes tribunal: first side to 3 juror votes wins losing side pays a small court fee → correct jurors stall / no review in time → creator wins (no trapped funds)

Payout started winner-take-all and was changed to proportionate reward (commit, 20 Feb) so a big staker doesn't dominate and small challengers still get a fair cut.

a deadline_checker resolves bets whose time has passed, so nothing sits half-finished.

AI moderation: two-stage, structured

The validator is a LangGraph StateGraph over an AgentState (title, criteria, amount → is_valid, reason). Groq's Llama 3.3 70B replaced the original local Ollama check for the hosted deploy.

Social graph & notifications

  • Follow graph gates challenges (you can only bet against people you follow) and powers the activity feed; a friends network was added in July.
  • Star system: community-curated feed sorted by popularity.
  • Notifications for challenges, proof requests, reviews, and verdicts; a proof-upload modal and review pages.
  • Admin dashboard (passphrase-gated) to view all users and bets.

What's tested

A broad backend suite: test_payouts, test_challenges, test_follows, test_notifications, test_failsafes (the no-trapped-funds guarantees), test_input_validation, test_llm_validator, test_admin, and auth/bet integration — the economy and its edge cases are the most-tested part.

The problem

Accountability works better with stakes and an audience. Project BAY is a social challenge platform: post a personal commitment ("I'll run 5km today"), stake virtual points, and let friends bet you won't do it.

Points are virtual, no money — a motivation game, not a betting app. The engineering interest is the economy: a fair pot, proportional payouts, a dispute jury, and AI moderation so people stake on real, safe commitments.

A challenge, start to finish

The economy & safety rules

  • Proportional payouts. Challengers win a share of the pot in proportion to their stake, not winner-take-all.
  • You can only challenge people you follow — the follow graph gates who can bet against whom.
  • Disputes go to a neutral jury. The first side to 3 votes decides; the winning side pays a small court fee to the jurors who called it right.
  • Funds are never trapped. New users start with 10 points; a stalled review auto-resolves to the creator.
  • AI moderation at creation. A bet must be personal, actionable and safe — not a prediction or abuse. A fast Groq check runs first; the full validation agent confirms.

The problem

Why is this road jammed? Upload fixed-camera footage, define the road, and get an evidence-first investigation: tracked subjects, a suspected cause, the frames that prove it, and a place for a human to record their review.

Built for Indian mixed traffic: configurable road regions/directions, relative movement (not uncalibrated speed), Indian plate formats (incl. BH-series).

the line the whole project is built around

This produces probabilistic incident assessments for human review. It must never be the sole basis for enforcement, identification, or accusations — and the code enforces that conservatism everywhere.

The investigation pipeline

What I observed / designed around

  • Good detection ≠ cause. A stopped car isn't a confirmed fault; the whole attribution layer is heuristic evidence strength, explicitly not calibrated probabilities.
  • Refuse to blame when unsure. Missing precedence/follower response caps a candidate below 0.5; no road regions caps below the 0.62 threshold; similar top scores → unknown.
  • Don't blame the queue for the jam. Queue followers are separated from the leader; earlier downstream stationary subjects reduce a candidate's precedence.
  • Tracking quality gates attribution, separately from detector confidence — a track that latched onto another object is rejected.
  • Indian specifics matter: autorickshaws aren't a COCO class (a documented gap); two-wheeler recall is the clearest weakness (17% in the 3D test).

honest evaluation

Five rendered 3D scenarios (IDM car-following, Blender CCTV) analysed by the real pipeline: stalled car ✓ 0.93, free flow ✓ no congestion, signal queue → unknown (two-wheelers missed), pedestrian missed (truck occlusion). Rendered scenes are cleaner than real cameras, so this proves the system works end-to-end, not field accuracy.
